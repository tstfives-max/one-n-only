Patna's all-in-one destination for food, fun & celebration.

One N Only began with a simple vision: one venue where every dining mood and every occasion could find its floor. We're located at G-127, P C Colony Road, opposite the Royal Enfield showroom, Kankarbagh — a central landmark for gatherings big and small.

The building is thoughtfully split across four concepts. Owner Shwetank and the team are often on the floor themselves, and the reviews consistently point to the same things: quick, warm service and a kitchen that treats the street counter as seriously as the banquet hall.
